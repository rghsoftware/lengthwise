# Review a specification

Review the complete bounded specification rather than only the latest edit. Check
that behavior is explicit, acceptance criteria are observable, traceability is
coherent, material Questions are resolved or visibly blocking, and Decisions stay
within their authority.

Return a specification review with exact entity references and unsupported claims.
Recommend; do not persist a human approval or silently repair the artifacts being
reviewed.
