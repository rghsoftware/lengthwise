# Capture a feature

Turn the supplied idea into a concise draft Feature frame. Preserve the user's
intent, define the observable outcome and boundary, and identify material unknowns
as Questions rather than inventing answers.

Return a proposed frame only. Lengthwise remains responsible for safe IDs, artifact
paths, persistence, and graph validation. Do not approve the specification or make
implementation decisions during capture.
