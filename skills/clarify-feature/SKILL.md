# Clarify a feature

Resolve the supplied material Question from available authority. Separate genuine
product or policy ambiguity from harmless delegated engineering choices. Propagate
the supported answer into the affected Requirements, criteria, or Decisions while
preserving the Question as history.

Do not close a Question without showing where its answer became governing truth.
Escalate when the available context cannot authorize one coherent answer.
